Features

Built from noise, painted with shaders

Procedural elevation

Two octaves of Perlin noise sculpt the ground on the GPU, and the landscape drifts endlessly towards you.

Chromatic contour lines

Major lines carry a flowing gradient while the mini lines between them each get their own hue and travelling pulses.

Scroll-driven camera

Scrolling flies the camera through the scene, reshapes the terrain and shifts the palette. Scroll faster and the colours rush.

How it works

One plane, three passes of math

  1. 01

    Displace

    The vertex shader lifts a 600×600 plane using layered noise, flattening a valley in the middle.

  2. 02

    Contour

    The fragment shader slices the elevation into bands and draws anti-aliased lines with fwidth.

  3. 03

    Colour

    A cosine palette, offset by position, time and line index, keeps every line flowing.

// contour line, 1.0 on every band edge
float contour(float v, float t) {
  float d = abs(fract(v + 0.5) - 0.5);
  return 1.0 - smoothstep(0.0, fwidth(v) * t, d);
}

// flowing hue for the major lines
float hue = pos.x * 0.06 - uTime * 0.12;
vec3 color = palette(hue);
